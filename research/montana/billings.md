# Billings, Montana — Permit Fee Research

**City:** Billings, Montana (Yellowstone County)
**Authority:** City of Billings — Building Division (Community Development), under Resolution 26-11315 (adopted February 23, 2026, effective on passage), which repealed Resolution 11-19068 (June 13, 2011)
**Research date:** 2026-09-26
**Researcher:** Permit Fee Intelligence — Montana pass

---

## 1. Authority

Billings sets every construction permit amount in a single stand-alone resolution:
**RES 26-11315, "Setting Building, Electrical, Plumbing, Mechanical, and Fire
System Permit Fees"**, passed unanimously 11-0 on **February 23, 2026**
(council agenda item: "PUBLIC HEARING AND RESOLUTION 26-11315 … Staff
recommends approval. APPROVED 11-0") and **effective on passage** per its own
§ 7. § 8 repeals all inconsistent resolutions including **Resolution 11-19068**
(June 13, 2011), the valuation-based schedule that had governed since 2011.

The 2026 resolution is a *fee reduction*: Building Division revenue had exceeded
projected expenditures, and the council cut every schedule — staff estimated
**23%–31% across the board**. The Gazette's account of the meeting records the
key structural changes: "a consolidation of the building permit fee schedule, a
reduction of commercial plan review fees from 65% to 60% and the elimination of
plan revision and deferred submittal fees", with residential projects no longer
carrying a plan review fee at all. A $2.5M commercial project drops from
~$11,010 to ~$8,246 (−25.1%). Department reserves are being drawn down toward
12 months of operating revenue (~$750K–$1M FY2027 revenue reduction), and staff
will review the fees each fiscal year.

## 2. Mechanism, by permit family

### 2.1 Building permit (one consolidated ladder, both occupancies)

§ 1 charges Residential and Commercial Building Permit fees "on the **higher of
total bid price values or based on the most recent valuation table established
in the International Code Council Building Safety Journal**" and then prints
**one schedule for both occupancies** — the consolidation the council coverage
describes:

| Total valuation | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| $1 – $2,000 | $45.00 |
| $2,001 – $25,000 | $45.00 for the first $2,000 plus $8.00 for each additional $1,000 or fraction |
| $25,001 – $50,000 | $229.00 for the first $25,000 plus $5.00 for each additional $1,000 or fraction |
| $50,001 – $100,000 | $354.00 for the first $50,000 plus $3.50 for each additional $1,000 or fraction |
| $100,001 – $500,000 | $529.00 for the first $100,000 plus $2.50 for each additional $1,000 or fraction |
| $500,001 – $1,000,000 | $1,529.00 for the first $500,000 plus $2.00 for each additional $1,000 or fraction |
| $1,000,001 – $5,000,000 | $2,529.00 for the first $1,000,000 plus $1.75 for each additional $1,000 or fraction |
| $5,000,001 and over | $9,529.00 for the first $5,000,000 plus $1.50 for each additional $1,000 or fraction |

**Seam check (the internal check on the read):** band 2 ends at $45 + 23 × $8.00
= $229.00 ✓; $229 + 25 × $5.00 = $354.00 ✓; $354 + 50 × $3.50 = $529.00 ✓; $529
+ 400 × $2.50 = $1,529.00 ✓; $1,529 + 500 × $2.00 = $2,529.00 ✓; $2,529 + 4,000
× $1.75 = $9,529.00 ✓. The ladder chains exactly at every seam.

**Plan review:** "COMMERCIAL PLAN REVIEW FEE SHALL BE ASSESSED AT 60% OF THE
PERMIT FEE" — printed as a single line under the schedule. **Residential has no
plan-review fee at all** under the 2026 resolution (the pre-2026 regime's
residential 65%-of-permit review was eliminated with the consolidation; the
Gazette draft carries no residential review line anywhere).

### 2.5 Fire system permit fees

§ 5, per system by project cost: $0–$500 → $45.00; $501–$1,000 → $90.00;
$1,001–$10,000 → $180.00; $10,001–$50,000 → $250.00; $50,001–$100,000 →

$75.00 per hour: inspections outside normal business hours (2-hour minimum);
reinspection (1-hour minimum); inspections with no specifically indicated fee
(1-hour minimum); additional plan review for changed plans (½-hour minimum).

## 3. Discrepancies and their resolution

1. **The adopted instrument is a scan.** The billingsmt.gov PDF has no text
   layer; its page 1 was read visually in the browser and matches the Gazette's
   published draft (a text-layer PDF of the same resolution) word for word on
   the building schedule and plan-review line. Amounts cite the draft's text
   with the page-1 identity check recorded here.
2. **Effective date:** the resolution's own § 7 says "effective immediately
   upon passage" (adopted 2026-02-23); the Gazette reports staff planning a
   March 2 go-live ("on or after March 2"). The seed uses the adoption date,
   2026-02-23, as `effectiveFrom` — the instrument's own term — and records the
   March 2 operational rollout in the research file.
3. **Residential plan review.** Pre-2026 reporting (and the 65%→60% reduction
   story) confirms the residential plan-review fee was **eliminated** in the
   2026 consolidation; the resolution prints only the commercial 60% line. The
   seed charges plan review only when the project is declared commercial.
4. **Multi-family electrical pricing** ("per building $100.00 + $40.00 per unit
   up to and including 12 units"; over 12 units under one roof uses the
   commercial project-cost ladder) is modelled with the per-unit row reading
   the units fact, capped by condition at 12 units.
5. **The plumbing schedule's interleaved extraction.** The scanned page's
   amount column interleaves in layout extraction; each row was matched to its
   amount by word coordinates (pymupdf), and the two genuinely ambiguous pairs
   (E/F and G/H) were resolved positionally — the coordinate dump is the
   documented read.

## 4. Worked examples (engine-verified 2026-09-26)

- **Commercial build-out, $650,000 valuation, commercial plan review.** Band 6:
  $1,529.00 + 150 × $2.00 = $1,829.00; plan review 60% = $1,097.40 →
  **$2,926.40** (the pre-2026 fee for the same project was ~$4,000+ under the
  65% regime — the reduction in action).
- **Residential new home, $320,000 valuation, no plan review.** Band 5: $529.00
  + 220 × $2.50 = **$1,079.00** flat.
- **Electrical (commercial), $60,000 project cost.** Band 4: $240.00 + 0.005 ×
  $10,000 = **$290.00**. Wait — $60,000 is in the "$50,001 or more" band: $440
  + 0.003 × $10,000 = **$470.00**.

## 5. What is modelled vs not

Modelled (three permit types): the consolidated building ladder (both
occupancies, one table) with the 60% commercial plan review; commercial
electrical's five-band project-cost ladder plus the residential flat rows with
their service-size/multi-family conditions; plumbing's $20.00 issuance plus the
fixture/sewer/water-heater/gas/backflow unit rows that map to engine per-unit
facts; the building page's worked examples.

Recorded but not modelled: fire system permits (§ 5's own seven-band project
cost table — a separate permit type); mechanical equipment rows (§ 4, recorded
row by row); moving-type rows and irrigation/wells electrical flats outside the
three pages' scope; the $75.00/hr event rows; the multi-family per-unit
electrical rows beyond the 12-unit condition; accessory-structure electrical
flats (recorded, available as data).
