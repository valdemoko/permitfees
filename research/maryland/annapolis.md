## Building permit fees (Title 17, building page)

- **Permit fees based on the estimated value of the work** (§17.05.130 et seq.):
  - $0–$500: **$25.00**
  - $501–$3,000: **$150.00**
  - $3,001–$5,000: **$175.00**
  - $5,001–$10,000: **$200.00**
  - $10,001 and more: **$250.00 + 0.8% of the estimated value in excess of $10,000.**
- **Nonrefundable application fee** based on estimated value: $25 ($0–$500),
  $100 ($501–$25,000), $150 ($25,001–$50,000), $200 ($50,001–$75,000),
  $200 + 0.25% over $75,000 ($75,001+).
- **Plan review / drawing submittal fee**, based on estimated cost of
  construction: $100 ($0–$10,000), $150 ($10,001–$15,000), $200
  ($15,001–$25,000), $350 ($25,001–$100,000), $350 + 0.1% over $100,000.
- Demolition/moving application fee: $100 residential, $150 commercial.
- The fee schedule prints ICC-style valuation guidance for dwellings elsewhere
  in Title 17; the calculator charges the value the applicant declares.

## Electrical permit fees (§17.16, electrical page)

- **A. New dwelling units:** 200 A service or less **$150.00**; more than 200 A
  **$150.00 plus $8.00 for each 100 amperes or fraction thereof in excess of 200 A.**
  New apartment dwelling units: 80% of the dwelling-unit fees. New
  nonresidential service equipment: ≤200 A $175; >200–300 $225; >300–400 $250;
  >400–1,200 $250 + $0.75/ampere over 400; >1,200 $1,250 + $2.50/ampere over 1,200.
- **B. Additions, alterations, repairs — rough wiring (outlets:** switches,
  lighting and receptacles each count as outlets**):** 1–10 outlets **$25**;
  11–40 **$50**; 41–75 **$75**; more than 75 **$10 per every 25 outlets or
  fraction thereof.** Rough wiring of fixtures: 1–10 **$25**; 11–40 **$50**;
  41–75 **$75**; >75 $10 per 25 or fraction.
- **C. All other work** (in addition to A and B): electrically operated signs
  $75; radio/TV receiving installation $50; reinspection $100/$150; failure to
  notify $50; investigation $75; electrical contractor's license $200/biennial.
- Solar photovoltaic: $10/module. Generators: $75 (1–8 kW), $30 per additional
  10 kW or fraction. Change in service size: $75.

## Plumbing fees (§17.28, plumbing page)

The plumbing section prices by **fixtures plus gas service pipe diameter**:

- **Gas service pipe charge** (including inspection by the City plumbing
  inspector): 2½" or less **$50.00**; 3" **$60.00**; 4" **$70.00**; 6" **$125.00**;
  8" **$250.00**; 10" **$350.00**; 12" **$500.00**.
- **Each fixture in addition to the first fixture: $10.00.** The first fixture's
  own amount is the gas-service-pipe row's minimum — the schedule reads
  "the sum of the fixture charges plus the amount of the applicable gas service
  pipe charges."
- **Installation charges:** first fixture **$60.00 residential / $80.00
  commercial**; each additional fixture connected to public sewer **$15.00**;
  private sewer **$30.00**; fixture omitted from original permit: $20/$40.
- Special fixture charges: grease trap $100; oil interceptor $100; water
  conditioning unit (single installation) $50; gas hot water heater (single
  installation) $40; inspection charge for water or sewer installation $100,
  reconstruct private sewer $50, A/C with water or drain connection $60;
  reinspection $100/$150; additional gas connection for gas water heaters $10.
- Master/journeyman plumber licenses: $200 biennially.

## Resolution of discrepancies

1. **Two-column layout confusion.** The FY26 PDF prints code sections and fee
   amounts in separate visual columns that flatten out of order in text
   extraction; the amounts above were re-aligned by reading the raw text
   against the printed page structure (e.g. "$60.00/first fixture" belongs to
   installation charges, not to capital facility assessments). Where two
   figures are printed adjacent (FY25 vs FY26 revisions), the later takes effect.
2. **Plumbing fixture pricing.** The schedule's plumbing permit is the sum of
   fixture charges and applicable gas-pipe charges; the calculator models the
   residential first fixture at $60.00 plus $15.00 per additional public-sewer
   fixture, which is the combination the schedule's installation-charge rows
   produce for a standard house permit, and states the assumption in the notes.
3. **Electrical outlet band edge.** "More than 75 outlets" charges $10 per
   every 25 or fraction — the band fee *adds* to the $75.00, per the schedule's
   cumulative reading of A and B.
