## 3. Mechanisms

### Building Permit
1. **New Single-Family Dwellings:**
   - Heated gross square footage is valued at **$15.00** per square foot.
   - Unheated gross square footage (garages, porches, unfinished basements) is valued at **$7.50** per square foot.
   - Formula: `((Heated_SqFt × 15.00) + (Unheated_SqFt × 7.50)) × 0.0055`.
   - Minimum permit fee: $50.00.
2. **Alterations, Additions & Commercial Projects:**
   - Formula: `Total Contract Valuation × 0.0055` ($5.50 per $1,000 of project valuation).
   - Minimum permit fee: $50.00.
3. **Plan Review Fee:**
   - Included or billed at standard review rates (typically 25% to 50% for complex commercial ePlans submissions).

### Electrical Permit
- Standalone trade permit issued to licensed electrical contractors.
- Minimum permit fee: $50.00.
- Formula: Base fee $50.00 plus tiered fee per circuit/ampacity, or 0.0055 of electrical subcontract valuation.

### Plumbing Permit
- Standalone trade permit issued to licensed plumbing contractors.
- Minimum permit fee: $50.00.
- Base schedule covering initial fixtures plus additional fixtures, water heaters, and sewer lateral connections.
