## 3. Mechanisms

### Building Permit
- **Valuation-based tiered rate:**
  - Residential work: $9.50 per $1,000 of valuation. Minimum fee of $125.00.
  - Commercial / Nonresidential work: $9.50 per $1,000 of valuation + $1.00 per $1,000 Craft Training Fund fee (total $10.50 per $1,000). Minimum fee of $125.00.
- **Plan Review Fee:** Projects requiring architectural/engineering review incur a plan review fee calculated at 50% of the building permit fee.

### Electrical Permit
- Standalone electrical permit pulled by state-licensed electrical contractor.
- Base inspection/minimum permit fee: $100.00.
- Valuation tier: $10.00 per $1,000 of electrical contract value for major works or flat scheduled item tiers.

### Plumbing Permit
- Standalone plumbing permit pulled by master plumber.
- Minimum permit fee: $100.00.
- Covers fixture replacements, new water service, sewer tap, and hot water heater installations.

## 4. Not Modelled
- Right-of-way street excavation permits (handled by City Engineering / Public Works).
- Environmental and grading permits under Title 4, Chapter 7.
- Fire suppression / sprinkler engineering fees (reviewed by Birmingham Fire and Rescue Service).
