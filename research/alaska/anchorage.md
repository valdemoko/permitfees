## 3. Mechanisms

### Building Permit
- **Residential Construction (IRC 1-3 family dwellings / Group R-3):**
  - Rate: 0.009 × valuation (0.9% of construction valuation).
  - Minimum permit fee: $360.00.
  - Plan review fee: 50% of the building permit fee.
- **Commercial Construction (IBC):**
  - $1.00 to $500,000: 0.015 × valuation (minimum fee: $525.00).
  - $500,001 to $1,000,000: 0.010 × valuation.
  - $1,000,001 to $5,000,000: 0.008 × valuation.
  - $5,000,001 and up: 0.006 × valuation.
  - Plan review fee: 65% of the building permit fee.

### Electrical Permit
- Standalone electrical permit pulled under AMC Chapter 23.10.
- Base inspection / permit fee: $175.00 (based on Table 3-C / Table 3-D standard inspection rate of $175.00/hour, 1-hour minimum).

### Plumbing Permit
- Standalone plumbing permit pulled under AMC Chapter 23.10.
- Base inspection / permit fee: $175.00 (based on Table 3-C / Table 3-E standard inspection rate of $175.00/hour, 1-hour minimum).

## 4. Not Modelled
