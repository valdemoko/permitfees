## 3. Mechanisms

### Building Permit
- **Valuation-based tiered ladder (Table 3-A):**
  - $1.00 to $500.00: $17.00
  - $500.01 to $2,000.00: $17.00 for the first $500 + $2.25 per additional $100 or fraction thereof
  - $2,000.01 to $25,000.00: $50.00 for the first $2,000 + $10.00 per additional $1,000 or fraction thereof
  - $25,000.01 to $50,000.00: $280.00 for the first $25,000 + $7.00 per additional $1,000 or fraction thereof
  - $50,000.01 to $100,000.00: $455.00 for the first $50,000 + $5.00 per additional $1,000 or fraction thereof
  - $100,000.01 to $300,000.00: $705.00 for the first $100,000 + $4.50 per additional $1,000 or fraction thereof
  - $300,000.01 to $500,000.00: $1,605.00 for the first $300,000 + $7.00 per additional $1,000 or fraction thereof
  - $500,000.01 to $1,000,000.00: $3,005.00 for the first $500,000 + $4.60 per additional $1,000 or fraction thereof
  - Over $1,000,000.00: $5,305.00 for the first $1,000,000 + $4.60 per additional $1,000 or fraction thereof
- **Plan Check Review Fee:** 75% of the Building Permit Fee (`BP × 0.75`).
- **Fire Review & Inspection Fee:** 15% of `(BP + PR)` for buildings without fire suppression/alarms; 20% of `(BP + PR)` for buildings with suppression & alarms.

### Electrical Permit
- Permit Issuance Fee: $35.00.
- Single-Family Dwellings: $255.00 flat fee (includes all wiring, service equipment, attached garages, and carports constructed at the same time).
- Duplex: $340.00 flat fee.
- Residential Additions: $85.00 flat fee (includes first 5 outlets/switches/lights; $3.00 per additional outlet).
- Plan Review Fee: 75% of electrical permit fee when plans are required.

### Plumbing Permit
- Permit Issuance Fee: $35.00.
- Unit Fee Schedule:
  - Each plumbing fixture: $15.00.
  - Water heater: $30.00.
  - Kitchen grease trap: $20.00.
  - Oil separator: $20.00.
- Plan Review Fee: 75% of plumbing permit fee when plans are required.
