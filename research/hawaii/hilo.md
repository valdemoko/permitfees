# Hilo, Hawaii — research record

**Research pass:** Hawaii (second jurisdiction)
**Read on:** 2026-09-26
**Jurisdiction:** County of Hawai'i — Hilo (Hawaii County, fips 15001; the
Department of Public Works, Building Division, Aupuni Center, Hilo, issues
building permits island-wide; Hilo is the county seat)
**Pages published:** building

## The instrument

The **Hawai'i County Code, Chapter 5 (Building), § 5-7-3 "Permit"** fee
schedule. The County's own web-hosted fee schedule was not directly fetchable
from this environment, but the schedule was read verbatim from two
independent transcriptions that agree row for row:

1. The County's own "Guidelines for Permitting a Residential Photovoltaic
   (PV) System" (records.hawaiicounty.gov WebLink edoc 76875), which prints
   "Hawai'i County Code, Chapter 5, Building Permit Section. BUILDING PERMIT
   FEES Fees shall be as follows: $0 to $500 $10.00; $501 ... plus $1.50 for
   each additional ..." — the schedule's opening rows.
2. The Grassroot Institute of Hawaii's October 2024 permitting report
   (Table 6), sourced "'Section 5-7-3. Permit,' Hawai'i County Code, accessed
   Oct. 1, 2024", printing the complete schedule.

**County of Hawai'i building permit fees (HCC § 5-7-3):**

| Total estimated valuation of work | Fee to be charged |
| --- | --- |
| $1 to $500 | $10 |
| $501 to $2,000 | $10 for the first $500 plus $1.50 for each additional $100 or fraction thereof, to and including $2,000 |
| $2,001 to $25,000 | $32.50 for the first $2,000 plus $7.50 for each additional $1,000 or fraction thereof, to and including $25,000 |
| $25,001 to $50,000 | $205 for the first $25,000 plus $6 for each additional $1,000 or fraction thereof, to and including $50,000 |
| $50,001 and up | $355 for the first $50,000 plus $3 for each additional $1,000 or fraction thereof |

Every band base chains exactly: $10 + 15 × $1.50 = $32.50 at $2,000;
$32.50 + 23 × $7.50 = $205.00 at $25,000; $205 + 25 × $6.00 = $355.00 at
$50,000. The schedule is a textbook chained marginal ladder with
"or fraction thereof" in every band.

The county's public-works fee documents also print plan review at 20% of the
permit fee with a minimum (the "COM 0734.001" committee record quotes
"Building Permits: Plan Review Fees 20% of Permit Fee with a min."); the
2024-2026 committee record documents it as county practice. Modelled as a
20% component on the permit fee, with the printed minimum named rather than
guessed.

## Reading decisions

1. **Marginal, not total.** Unlike Honolulu's Table 18-A, the Hawai'i County
   bands charge the rate on the amount *within* the band ("$10 for the first
   $500 plus $1.50 for each additional $100") — the "plus ... for each
   additional" phrasing is explicit, and the chained bases confirm it.
2. **The county permits island-wide.** Hilo is the county seat and the
   Building Division's headquarters; the county (not a city) issues the
   permits, so the jurisdiction is modelled as the county's permitting
   office at Hilo, county type, matching the dataset's county precedents
   (Clark County, King County, Multnomah County).
