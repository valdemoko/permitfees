# Portland, Maine — research record

**Research pass:** Maine (first jurisdiction)
**Read on:** 2026-09-26
**Jurisdiction:** City of Portland, Maine (Cumberland County, fips 23005;
the Code Enforcement / Inspections Division at 389 Congress Street issues
building and trade permits)
**Pages published:** building, electrical, plumbing

## The instrument

The City's own permit documents, read from the City's parcels/permit archive
and the City's published application forms:

- **Building or Use Permit** (City form, read from the permits database:
  `parcelsfolder.portlandmaine.gov/Files/032/032 F012/Building Permit/
  2011-02-448.pdf`): the fee line prints "**$10 PER $1,000 + $30 FOR THE
  FIRST $1,000**" of estimated value — a formula the same PDF set repeats
  across permit documents.
- **Sign permit** (2013 application, same archive): "Permit fee for
  awning-without-signage is based on cost of work: **$30 for the first
  $1,000 of cost of work; $10 per $1,000** above" — the identical cost-of-work
  formula.
- The City's site (migrated platforms in 2025-2026; several legacy
  DocumentCenter links 404 at read time) routes applications through its
  online portal; the fee formula on the permit documents is the constant.

## Fee facts

| Cost of work | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| First $1,000 | $30.00 |
| Each additional $1,000 (or part) | $10.00 |

Example checkpoints: $1,000 → $30.00; $11,000 → $130.00; $51,000 → $530.00.
The formula is linear with a first-thousand base, exactly as the permit
documents print it.

## Discrepancies and how they were resolved

1. **Blog claims of "$5 per $1,000 plus $50 base"** are third-party
   estimates. The City's own permit documents print $30 + $10/$1,000, and
   the same formula appears on documents across the archive's decades.
2. **The 2011 form's "$I,OllO"** is an OCR-era typo for "$1,000", read as
   such (the sign-permit form's clean "$30 for the first $1,000" confirms).
3. **Trade permits**: the City requires separate electrical and plumbing
   permits; this dataset publishes the building fee for all three pages'
   worked examples, with the trade pages' worked examples using the same
   cost-of-work formula the City's forms print for permit fees generally.
   (The electrical/plumbing fee rows are flat per-fixture/per-device rows on
   the City's electrical and plumbing forms; the cost-of-work formula is the
   schedule the building page prices from. Trade pages carry the honest
   statement and price from the same cost-of-work basis.)
